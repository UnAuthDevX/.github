By using the App, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. Where applicable, we will request your consent separately for optional data collection, such as analytics.

## 1. Information Collection and Storage

### A. Local Storage

Our App uses local device storage, such as AsyncStorage, an internal database, or shared preferences, to store application data locally on your device.

This data may include:

* In-app progress, task statuses, reward points, and balance tracking.
* User preferences and app settings.
* Financial records and information entered by you, such as transactions, bills, budgets, or related information.
* Cached session data and other application-related information.

**Important:** This application data is primarily stored locally on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to or stored on our own external private servers without your explicit knowledge.

If you clear the App's cache or data, reset the App, or uninstall the App, locally stored information may be permanently erased.

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Dream Paisa uses third-party services to provide advertising, analytics, and related functionality.

These services may collect and process certain information from your device according to their respective privacy policies.

### A. Google AdMob

We use **Google AdMob** to display advertisements in the App.

To provide and personalize advertisements, limit repetitive advertisements, detect fraud, measure advertising performance, and improve its services, Google AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information from your device, including:

* **Advertising Identifier:** Google Advertising ID (AAID / GAID), where available.
* **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen size, carrier, language settings, and similar technical information.
* **Location Data:** Approximate or coarse location derived from information such as IP address, which may be used to serve region-relevant advertisements.
* **Diagnostics and Performance Data:** App performance information, crash-related information, and advertising interaction metrics such as clicks and impressions.

The information collected by Google and its partners is subject to their respective privacy policies and practices.

For more information, please review:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google Uses Information From Sites or Apps That Use Their Services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* [Google AdMob Policies](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543)

### B. Firebase Analytics

Dream Paisa may use **Google Firebase Analytics** to understand how users interact with the App and to improve the App's functionality, performance, reliability, and user experience.

When Firebase Analytics is enabled, it may automatically collect information such as:

* App launches and sessions.
* Screens and features accessed.
* Interactions with App features and events.
* App version and operating system information.
* Device model and technical information.
* Approximate geographic information.
* Analytics identifiers and related identifiers.
* Usage, diagnostic, and performance information.
* Other information automatically collected by Firebase Analytics according to Google's applicable policies.

### Analytics Consent

Firebase Analytics is treated as an **optional analytics service**.

Where the App presents an analytics consent request, you can choose whether to allow analytics collection.

If you select **"Allow"**, Firebase Analytics may be enabled and collect the information described above.

If you select **"Don't Allow"**, Firebase Analytics collection will remain disabled, and Dream Paisa's core functionality will continue to be available.

Your decision regarding optional analytics does not prevent you from using the core financial tracking features of Dream Paisa.

Dream Paisa does not intentionally send individual financial records, transaction amounts, account balances, or other manually entered financial records to Firebase Analytics as analytics data.

Analytics data may nevertheless contain information about interactions with App features, depending on the events configured in the App.

You may be provided with controls within the App to review or change your analytics preference where supported.

For more information, please review Google's applicable privacy documentation:

* [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
* [Google Analytics for Firebase](https://firebase.google.com/docs/analytics)

## 3. Managing Advertising and Analytics Choices

### A. Advertising Preferences

You have control over how your device's advertising identifier and personalized advertising are used.

Depending on your Android version and device manufacturer, you may be able to:

#### Reset Advertising ID

Reset your Google Advertising ID through your Android device's advertising settings.

Typically, this can be found under:

**Settings → Google → Ads**

The exact settings and names may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

#### Delete Advertising ID or Opt Out

On supported Android versions, you may be able to delete your Advertising ID or disable personalized advertising through your device's advertising settings.

When you opt out of personalized advertising, you may still see advertisements, but those advertisements may be less relevant to your interests.

### B. Analytics Choice

Where analytics consent is presented within Dream Paisa:

* **Allow Analytics** — Firebase Analytics may collect analytics information as described in this Privacy Policy.
* **Don't Allow** — Firebase Analytics collection remains disabled.

Declining analytics does not disable the core functionality of Dream Paisa.
