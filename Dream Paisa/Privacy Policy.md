## 3. Managing and Opting Out of Targeted Ads

You have control over how your device's advertising identifier and personalized advertising are used.

Depending on your Android version and device manufacturer, you may be able to:

### Reset Advertising ID

Reset your Google Advertising ID through your Android device's advertising settings.

Typically, this can be found under:

**Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID**

### Delete Advertising ID or Opt Out

On supported Android versions, you may be able to delete your Advertising ID or disable personalized advertising through your device's advertising settings.

When you opt out of personalized advertising, you may still see advertisements, but those advertisements may be less relevant to your interests.

The exact settings and names may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.
